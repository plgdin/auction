# Hyperframes Composition Brief: Lelam (Director's Cut)

## Objective
Create a high-impact, vertical launch brag video (1080x1920, 24.5s) for Lelam (lelam.co), India's multi-portal eAuction intelligence platform, based on the Tendril/Apple minimalist motion aesthetic.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: vertical (1080x1920)
- Duration: 24.5s (GSAP Timeline)
- Voiceover: `assets/voiceover_v2.wav` (22.9s Kokoro speech)
- Music: `assets/music/bgm.mp3`

## Visual Identity
- Dark Terminal Slate: `#070b13` to `#0f172a`
- Primary Accent: `#3b82f6` (Cobalt Electric Blue)
- Portal Badges:
  - MSTC: `#f59e0b`
  - BaankNet: `#10b981`
  - GeM: `#8b5cf6`
  - Profit / ROI: `#22c55e`
- Botanical Scene: Minimalist Japanese Bonsai branch (`assets/sakura.jpg`)
- Meadow Outro: Photorealistic wildflower cosmos meadow (`assets/meadow.jpg`)

## Scene Breakdown
1. **Scene 1 (0.0s - 3.8s):** The Fragmentation Hook (`too many portals`, scattering acronyms).
2. **Scene 2 (3.8s - 9.0s):** Multi-Portal Synapse Web (3 Live Auction Cards: MSTC, BaankNet, GeM).
3. **Scene 3 (9.0s - 14.5s):** Landed Cost Engine (Base ₹45L, Taxes + Freight ➔ Landed ₹54.35L ➔ +24.8% Margin).
4. **Scene 4 (14.5s - 19.5s):** Wealth Tree / Valuation Dossier (Bonsai Branch with blooming outcome badges).
5. **Scene 5 (19.5s - 24.5s):** Wildflower Meadow Horizon & Brand Outro ("One place to track. One place to Bid." ➔ Lelam Logo & URL).

## Audio
- Voiceover: `assets/voiceover_v2.wav` on track index 2, starts at 0.3s, volume 1.0.
- BGM: `assets/music/bgm.mp3` on track index 1, volume 0.30, ducking during VO, smooth fade-out at end.
- Tactical UI SFX: Key drops, switch snaps, cash register/bell chime, and closing gong.
