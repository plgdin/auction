# Brag Plan: Lelam (Director's Cut)

## What is this app?
Lelam (lelam.co) is India's multi-portal eAuction intelligence platform aggregating MSTC industrial scrap auctions, BaankNet SARFAESI bank property foreclosures, and GeM government tenders into one unified bidding analytics terminal.

## The angle
In India, government eAuctions and bank liquidations have historically been trapped in an opaque, fragmented maze: buried across disparate portal interfaces, obscure newspaper notices, and unreadable tender PDFs with hidden taxes. 
This film portrays the transition from **portal overload and hidden risk** into **real-time intelligence, automated landed cost calculation, and institutional clarity**.
