## 1. Creative Premise & Narrative Arc

In India, government eAuctions and bank liquidations have historically been trapped in an opaque, fragmented maze: buried in chaotic portal interfaces, obscure newspaper notices, and unreadable tender PDFs with hidden taxes. 

This film portrays the transition from **portal overload and hidden risk** into **real-time intelligence, automated landed cost calculation, and institutional clarity**:

```
[ACT I: THE OVERLOAD]       "too many portals" ➔ Scattered acronyms & tender PDFs
        │
[ACT II: THE ROUTING]       Electric blue pulse splits into MSTC, BaankNet & GeM
        │
[ACT III: CONVERGENCE]      3D auction cards vortex into the Lelam Intelligence Terminal
        │
[ACT IV: FINANCIAL ENGINE]  Live terminal executing Landed Cost (GST 18%, TCS 1%, Logistics) ➔ +24.8% Margin
        │
[ACT V: THE WEALTH BLOOM]   Chaotic bid data organizes into a blossoming golden Bonsai Tree ("LELAM DOSSIER")
        │
[ACT VI: HORIZON RESOLVE]   Cinematic tracking shot into natural daylight: "One place to Bid." ➔ Lelam.co
```

## 5. Compositing Guidelines for Motion Designers

1. **Easing Curves:**
   * UI Snap: `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (snappy 0.4s entrance, 0.8s hold).
   * Badge Bounce: `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`.
2. **Audio-Visual Precision:**
   * Every number calculation change and checkmark spawn must hit within **1 frame (16.6ms)** of the audio Foley effect.
3. **Typography:**
   * Display Font: `Inter` or `Archivo` (bold, institutional).
   * Data & Calculations: `Geist Mono` or `JetBrains Mono` for tabular number alignment.
